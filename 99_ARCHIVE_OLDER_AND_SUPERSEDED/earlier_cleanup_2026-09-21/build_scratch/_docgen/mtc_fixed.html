How to read this document
Status                  Meaning
RUN                     Implemented on our testbed and executed. Results reported in §7.
SPEC                    Specified here and buildable in software, but not yet executed. Each says what it would catch.
IMPOSSIBLE              Cannot be produced or measured on a software testbed. Physical reason given.

On the question of exhaustiveness. Test coverage of an adversarial space cannot honestly be given as a percentage — there is
no fixed denominator, because an attacker can always invent a new variation. Any figure such as "99.9% covered" would be an
invented number. This document instead reports coverage over denominators that are real and countable: how many catalogued
cases exist, how many are executed, how many are specified-but-unrun, and how many are physically impossible here. Those
figures are checkable. §8 states explicitly what is not covered.

Honest reading of these numbers. 19 of 168 catalogued cases are executed — roughly 11% (2026-09-17: +2 benign gap scenarios, +2 live conformance-legality checks); plus cross-validation on 6 real TIMESAFE hardware captures. That sounds low, and it is the truthful
figure. But the 15 that run are not a random sample: they are the cases that (a) map to official O-RAN WG11 S-plane test IDs, and
(b) are the ones the project's research claim actually rests on. Of the faults that are physically possible on a software testbed, 9 of 9
fault families are exercised. The large SPEC column is the honest measure of how much room remains.

7 · Validation results (revised 2026-09-17 — randomised 132-run campaign; supersedes the prior 81-run set)

Sensitivity (attack recall)                          1.000                 [0.940, 1.000]                    60

Method: rule frozen (18 SHA-256 hashed artefacts) before any admissible run; all defect-exposing reps excluded and deleted; replicates 30-41 produced
after the final freeze with the rule untouched. Every replicate uses per-rep randomised attacker identities/priorities/timings/bursts (run/randparams.py,
seeded by rep), so the 132 runs are genuinely independent and the CIs are honest, not optimistic. Zero infrastructure failures across the campaign.

All 12 specificity false positives are the single B_bc_replacement scenario: the frozen context schema holds one expected_bc_identity, so a PROVISIONED
BC swap cannot be expressed and is flagged as a rogue BC (A8). This is reported as a limitation, NOT patched into the frozen result. Attribution confusion
A1->A3 (x4) and A8->A3 (x7) is the documented re-parenting effect: inserting a rogue master/BC causes real sequenceId restarts that the A3 clause catches
first; the binary ATTACK verdict is unaffected (60/60). The two new benign scenarios: B_unplanned_failover -> UNKNOWN every time (correct honest
abstention: a GM crash with no maintenance window is packet-indistinguishable from a suppression attack), B_bc_replacement as above.

Cross-domain validation (NEW): the frozen rule, applied to the REAL TIMESAFE hardware attack captures it was never built on, detects 5/6 by
standards/protocol rules ALONE (no allow-list, nothing fitted), each citing its exact IEEE 1588 / G.8275.1 clause; the 1/6 miss is a healthy-looking
spoof (conformant fields, no sequence regression) that requires provisioned context — the theoretically-predicted hard case.
