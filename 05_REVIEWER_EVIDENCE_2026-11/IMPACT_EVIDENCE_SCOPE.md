# Impact evidence scope for the eight S-plane attacks

## Evidence boundary

**MEASURED** means observed in this repository's software testbed. The 168-run campaign executed 14 scenarios × 12 repetitions; its primary evaluation is `01_CURRENT_SPlane_SelfHealing/oran_splane_selfhealing/gap_coverage_2026-09-20/corrected_final/EVALUATION_V4.json`. The later recovery evaluation executed 14 scenarios × 2 arms × 5 repetitions and used a passive `pmc -d 24` observer (`03_RECOVERY_LOOP_S-PLANE/RESULTS_2026-10-05.md`).

All daemons ran with `free_running 1`. Therefore, protocol relationships—parent, grandmaster, port state, duration and restoration—are measured, but physical time error and user service are not. **REFERENCE** impacts below come from stored standards or literature and must not be read as outcomes of this testbed.

## Cross-check of the 5 October documents

The `Source`, `Destination`, `Attack devices` and `Consequence` fields are textually identical for A1, A2, A3, A5 and A8 across:

- `ORAN_Fault_Detectability_v2026-10-05.xlsx` (`DETECTABILITY`),
- `ORAN_SPlane_Attack_vs_Benign_Classification_v2026-10-05.xlsx` (`ATTACK catalog`), and
- `ORAN_SPlane_Parameter_Fault_Matrix_v2026-10-05.xlsx` (`FAULT KEY`).

C1–C3 appear with these four fields in `ORAN_SPlane_Packets_to_Classification_2026-10-05.xlsx` (`PER-RUN`); there is no second 5 October four-column row set against which to do a textual equality check. The following substantive disagreements or scope corrections remain:

1. **A8:** older wording that all RU logs “took” the rogue BC as master is superseded. The 5 October workbooks correctly say 36/36 recorded a foreign-master candidate and 5/5 later `pmc` runs kept the real parent.
2. **C3:** the campaign-era `ptp4l` selection-line interpretation said no master change; the 5 October `pmc -d 24` observer supersedes it and shows re-parenting in 5/5. The latest packet/classification workbook carries the corrected wording.
3. **C2:** the documents correctly identify an ETSI unexpected-input test family, but the exact `versionPTP 3 / reserved type / short length` combination is project-defined and not the ETSI procedure. This is a citation-scope disagreement, not a measured-data disagreement.
4. **Service consequence columns:** several cells mix project measurement with standards/literature consequences. Only the clauses explicitly tagged `MEAS`/`REC` are measured here; cell drop, inaccurate physical time and RF/service degradation remain **REFERENCE**.
