## Best Reading Path for Your Literature Survey

1. Architecture grounding: 3GPP/ETSI/O-RAN standards in `papers/Standards`.
2. O-RAN digital twin industry direction: O-RAN DT-RAN use cases and key enablers.
3. Testbed and validation: Colosseum, OpenRAN Gym, TwinRAN, Gen-TWIN.
4. AI-native control loops: dApps, rApps, AI-RAN, agentic O-RAN.
5. Fault/anomaly focus: Federated continual learning for O-RAN anomaly detection, cross-domain AI attack detection, adversarial 6G AI survey.
6. Monetizable NOC angle: NDT for end-to-end 6G, slicing, energy-saving rApps, SLA-aware self-healing.
